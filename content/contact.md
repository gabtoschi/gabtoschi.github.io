# Frequently asked questions
Maybe I already have the answer you are looking for!
<br /><br />

### Can I hire you as a software engineer/front-end web developer?
Well, it's my job for 10+ years after all! I'm already working 40h/week as a developer, and have a lot of games to create, but if you have a cool project, why not? **HTML + CSS + JavaScript** is where I shine, I've had experiences with mostly **all frameworks** in the market (React, Vue, Angular, Eleventy) and learning new things is not a problem. Get in touch!

### Do you work with digital games as well?
I started my game design journey on digital games and I majored in Computer Science because of that, so, I **would love to go back** someday in interesting projects. Probably if the game is closer to a board game, I think you will trust me more and I'll be more comfortable from the start, but **puzzles** are also a specialty of mine.

### Can I license one of your published games in my country?
**Yes, please!** Most of my games were **published only in Brazil**, so I would love to see those designs travelling the world. If it is a title that I still have license outside the country, we can talk directly. If not, I can help connect you with the original publisher.

### Do you can give a talk/workshop for my company/community?
Sometimes, people need to ask me to stop talking! I would **love to prepare a talk** or workshop and share (and learn) more about game design with the world. Please, send me a message and we can schedule!

### Can I invite you for a interview/podcast/internet thingy?
Again, I'll never refuse an opportunity to talk a lot. Just send me a message and we can schedule something. I'm comfortable with Portuguese and English, and I can try my rusty Spanish if you ask me.

### Do you really can design a game for my brand/IP/specific demand?
**Yes!** For some time now, I'm working close with publishers to design games that can fit those specific needs. **I can adapt my process** depending of how your company works, and we can find together a way to make great games that will fit your catalog. [**The Jetsons**](/games/jetsons) is a great example of a design I created closely for an IP!

### Do you want to co-design a game with me?
At least half of the games on my workbench right now are being designed with other amazing people, so **I'm always looking forward new partners**. Of course, if we don't know each other yet, it can be a little bit awkward on the beginning, but send me an idea and we can see how it goes from there.

### Can I send you a game key to get a review/social post?
Right now, I'm **focusing more on creating new games** instead of producing content. That said, I'll probably find a new medium to talk about them in the future, but it'll probably be focused on recommendations and design discussions than reviews, so I can't promise content for anything you send to me. *(Writing about games that I didn't liked was one of the reasons I stopped writing for a while, to be honest)*

If that's OK to you and your team, just get in touch! At least, I can provide some personal feedback about the game as a player and a designer.
