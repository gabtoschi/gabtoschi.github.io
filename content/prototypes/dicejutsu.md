**Throw your dice** across the box, tumbling rivals while keeping your own with high-numbered pips. Use dice with **special powers** to change the course of the battle and dominate the arena's special area to secure your place in the fight. *Co-designed with [Danilo Valente](https://manganacarta.com/).*
