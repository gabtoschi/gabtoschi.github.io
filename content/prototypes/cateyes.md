A dealer starts throwing cards around, one at a time, and no time to count 'em. All have eyes, and **those eyes can be cats or robots**. The moment you believe there are 10+ cats in front of you, slap the table! *Co-designed with [Danilo Valente](https://manganacarta.com/).*
