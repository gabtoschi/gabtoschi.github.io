<!--section:intro-->
*Birthday parties are the best! But only eating sweets at the end is no fun!* A fast-paced game of attention and memory about the most **famous sweets at Brazilian birthday parties**. Instead of waiting for the "Happy Birthday" song, you have to rush to the sweets table along with the others, trying to collect the best assortment of treats to win.

<!--section:desc-->
In each round, everyone chooses a number with their hand and reveals it at the same time, indicating "how fast they will run" to the sweets table. **Whoever chose the same number bumps into each other and gets no candy!** And if you're not careful, someone will rat you out and you'll be left without any sweets! Every type of candy scores in a different way, so getting a lot isn't always the best choice. When all cards were distributed, the game ends and whoever gets the highest final score wins!

The game was launched as part of the **Volume 2 of the Piquinim Collection**, an independent 18-card game anthology inspired by Brazilian themes and culture. As a stretch goal for the crowdfunding campaign, the "Party Kit" expansion was also released, allowing 5-player matches and introducing new card types, with new dynamics.

# Behind the design process
My story with this game started way back, on my efforts on re-learning game design during the pandemic. The idea was to implement a version of the **Prisoner's Dillema that would work for 3+ people** in a simple and fun way, but the original prototype had 100+ cards, 1 die, 1 meeple and too much problems to solve. That said, "stealing candies" theme started here too, as **an homage to my mom**, whose moments making sweets for my birthdays sweetened my childhood.

Years later, when the "18 cards" bug bited me, I decided to rework the experience in a smaller game, and **it worked really well!** Brigadeiro was my first choice when we started working on the Piquinim Collection, but because of a publisher that strung me along for months for no reason, it was only published on Volume 2. This was also an opportunity to work on a commercial game with [Hugo Pereira](https://www.instagram.com/itshugosnook/), one of my oldest friends and a great illustrator, and I always feel proud of us when I see our names together in the box.
